Questions

What sales leaders ask before they apply.

Is this a training course?

No. It's a working session for the person who leads the team. You diagnose your own team and leave with one change to make, not a lecture on AI tools.

What if my team doesn't have AI tools yet?

The workshop assumes your team can use at least one general AI assistant. If they can't, the business case you build in the session can include getting them.

Can I bring a colleague?

Yes, and it helps. Two or more managers from one company get a team rate, and a change sticks better when the people who approve it were in the room.

What if it isn't right for me?

Seats are booked through Maven and covered by the Maven Guarantee. The short application also lets us tell you honestly if it isn't a fit before you pay.
