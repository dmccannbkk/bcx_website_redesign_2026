How BCX Intelligence works

Three phases. One diagnostic. A decision-ready output.

Powered by Kindling — BCX Design's proprietary diagnostic product.

01 Alignment

Establish a clear picture from the inside

Before any audit begins, decision owners and key stakeholders answer a structured survey. Our behavioural AI engine analyses the responses and surfaces a summary. Leadership comes together for a focused session to align around what the data is showing and what question the audit needs to answer.

02 Behavioural Audit

Find what the dashboards are not showing

We examine the customer or employee experience through a behavioural science lens — looking for the friction points your dashboards and surveys are not designed to find. Hidden operational failures, cognitive barriers to decision-making, gaps between what people are expected to do and what the system actually makes easy. The audit produces a behavioural diagnosis: where the friction is, why it is there, and the interventions most likely to address it.

03 Decision Workshop

Turn findings into decisions

Working with your cross-functional team, we prioritise the interventions that will produce the most significant impact, sequence them practically, and establish what success looks like. The output is a decision-ready plan, not a research deck.

Common questions

"We already have data."

Data tells you what happened. It records outcomes — conversion rates, cycle times, adoption figures, NPS scores. What it cannot tell you is why people made the decisions that produced those outcomes. That gap — between what the numbers show and what is actually driving them — is exactly where BCX Intelligence works.

"We already have consultants."

Most consulting engagements start with the problem the client has already named. BCX Intelligence starts earlier — with whether that problem is the right one to solve. If your existing partners are working from the same diagnosis you arrived at before they walked in, the quality of their recommendations is limited by the quality of that diagnosis.

"Isn't this what our Head of CX or Strategy should be doing?"

Possibly. But most internal teams are too close to the problem, too invested in existing assumptions, and too constrained by internal politics to surface the real friction objectively. BCX Intelligence gives them a clearer, debiased picture to work from — which makes their work more effective, not redundant.
